### Hi, I'm Rodney 👋

**Rodney Cuevas, CCM, BCES** · Manager, Air Quality Management Branch, MDEQ

I build the tools I wish existed for air quality work, mostly in **R Shiny** and **Next.js**, on public data from EPA, NOAA and the NWS.
